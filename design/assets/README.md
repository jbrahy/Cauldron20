## The emblem

Three weathered skulls over a downturned sword, flanked by crossed maces and a
twenty-sided die, set on a crimson lozenge — a dark, gritty D&D group crest in a
bone-and-gold palette on black. On the parchment landing page it is framed as a
**dark, gold-bordered plaque/medallion** so the black artwork reads as intentional.

## Motto

> **Astu non vi** — *"by cunning, not by force."*

## Notes

- The page frames the emblem on a dark fill (`#0e0a05`) with a gold border so its
  black background blends seamlessly into the parchment design.
- At very small sizes (footer/ribbon) the detail compresses to a dark crest; that's
  expected for a painted raster mark.
- Cauldron VTT itself is free, open-source software (GPL-2.0) by **Hugo Leisink**
  (https://www.cauldron-vtt.net/); this brand applies only to the League's instance.
