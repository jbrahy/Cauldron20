# Campaigns

Ten original, ready-to-run tabletop campaign design documents (5e-compatible, system-agnostic flavor — no copyrighted stat blocks). Each is a self-contained, table-ready doc (~3,000–4,200 words) following the same 16-section structure: at-a-glance summary, premise & hooks, setting, themes, factions, key NPCs, locations, act structure, signature encounters, mysteries & twists, an antagonist "doom clock," treasure, session-zero notes, running tips, and random tables.

Deliberately varied across level range, tone, and structure so the set works as a whole.

## Loglines

- **01 · The Hollow Crown** — A king's golden age was borrowed against tomorrow, and the lender from the Court of Thorns has come to collect — in seasons, in memory, and in blood-of-the-crown.
- **02 · Tide of the Drowned God** — The sea rises because something below is finally turning over in its sleep, and dreaming your name.
- **03 · Ash & Iron** — The forge-god is dying, and an empire of living fire learns too late that faith does not burn as long as coal.
- **04 · The Clockwork Pilgrimage** — A train so long it threads through realities is uncoupling into the void, and something aboard wants the pilgrimage to fail forever.
- **05 · Whispers in the Verdant Wound** — The forest is not dying — it is healing, and that is the horror.
- **06 · Crown of Cinders** — A buried dragon-queen of ash is waking; you begin as nobodies and end at the last bridge with the world's only torch.
- **07 · The Last Caravan** — Forty days until the Ash Road closes forever, one caravan left, across a desert that remembers everyone who has ever crossed it.
- **08 · Hex of the Pale Court** — The sun set a hundred years ago and never finished rising; the Baroness keeps her larder warm, and there is no longer a road out.
- **09 · Stars Below** — Miners struck a stone sky full of stars that watch back; descend before the surface starts dreaming the same dream.
- **10 · The Merchant Princes of Sael** — In a canal-city of feuding houses, climb the ladder of favors and knives before a hidden conspiracy buys the whole city out from under everyone.
