# Whispers in the Verdant Wound

> The Thornmere Wood is not dying. It is healing — and that is the horror. Where steel once scarred it, new flesh-bark grows hungry, and the old forest dreams a green dream of a world without the wound that bleeds. They call it the Verdant Wound. It is learning your name.

## Premise & The Hook

Eleven years ago, the loggers of **Hollowstand** drove an iron rail-spur into the heart of the Thornmere to feed the smelters downriver. They cut a clearing the locals named the **Felling Scar**. The forest tried to heal it. Something answered the trying.

Now a creeping green called the **Verdant Wound** spreads outward from the Scar in a slow tide. It does not kill the way fire or frost kills. It *grafts*. Deer wake with bark for ribs. A trapper's hand fuses to the haft of his axe and the axe puts out leaves. Wounds — any wound, in anything — sprout new and hungry life that remembers it was once a deeper hurt.

**The hook:** Hollowstand has hired the party to escort a sealed wagon of "cure" — an alchemist's blight-salve — to the trapped outpost of **Greenhollow Watch**, three days into the deepening green. The wagon never needed escorting from bandits. It needed escorting from the forest. By the second night, one of the oxen has grown a second, weeping mouth along its flank, and it is singing.

## Setting & Atmosphere

The Thornmere is an old, cold, broadleaf forest gone wrong in concentric rings. At the **healthy verge** it merely feels watched. Deeper, the **bloom-line** begins: moss that flushes warm-red when you bleed near it, fungus that grows toward your heartbeat, and the smell — copper and crushed mint and the sweet rot of a healing wound under a bandage left too long.

Past the bloom-line lies the **Grafting**: trees with the grain of muscle, sap that clots like blood, gall-pods big as a man that twitch when you pass. Things here are *almost* finished growing. A wolf with antlers of fingerbone. A songbird whose wings are two leaves and whose breast is one staring eye. They are not undead. They are not demons. They are the forest's first drafts, and the forest is a patient editor.

At the center, in the Felling Scar, is the **Heartwood Sore** — a swelling green mound that breathes, four hundred feet across, warm to the touch, and threaded with the rusted bones of the old rail-spur like a splinter the body grew around but never expelled.

Sensory anchors: bark that gives like skin under a press; the wet click of pods opening; birdsong played a half-second too late, as though echoed; and always the whisper — leaves moving with no wind, shaping the same three syllables, almost a word, almost your name.

## Themes & Tone

This is a tragedy, not a monster hunt. The Wound is not evil; it is a wounded thing healing the only way it knows, and *every* cure the party can offer is also a kind of killing. Run it slow and quiet. Let dread come from beauty as much as gore — a meadow of flowers that turn to track the warmest body, a stag that lays its head in a paladin's lap and weeps sap. Reserve the worst body-horror for moments the party *earns* by going deeper. The forest should feel like a patient that can't be saved without amputation, and the party are the surgeons holding the saw.

## Factions & Powers

**The Circle of the Unbroken Bough** (druid splinter — *restorationists*)
- **Goal:** Heal the Wound by *guiding* it, not stopping it — let the forest finish its work cleanly.
- **Methods:** Ritual, sacrifice of self and willing beasts, slow patient grafting of their own bodies into the green.
- **Secret:** Their leader is already three-quarters merged with the Heartwood Sore and no longer fully herself. They are not negotiating for the forest; the forest is negotiating through them.
- **Reaction:** Wary welcome that curdles to grief if the party speaks of burning or cutting.

**The Circle of the Salted Root** (druid splinter — *cauterizers*)
- **Goal:** Kill the Wound utterly before it reaches the river and the lowland farms.
- **Methods:** Salt-fire, blight-bane fungal toxins, controlled burns, killing every grafted thing as a mercy.
- **Secret:** Their methods are accelerating the spread — the Wound treats their burns as fresh injuries and heals *bigger*. They suspect this and have stopped telling each other.
- **Reaction:** Recruiting, urgent, suspicious of anyone who's been deep and come back changed.

**The Hollowstand Concord** (frontier town)
- **Goal:** Survive the season, keep the smelter contracts, not become refugees.
- **Methods:** Quarantine, bounties on green-touched folk, hiring outsiders (the party) to do what they're too frightened to.
- **Secret:** The town magistrate ordered the original deep cut that woke the Wound, and has been burning the survey records that prove it.
- **Reaction:** Desperate, transactional, increasingly willing to burn its own neighbors.

**The Verdant Wound** (the blight-mind itself)
- **Goal:** Finish healing. It does not know it is hurting anything; pain is data it grafts around.
- **Methods:** Spread, adapt, *learn* — it samples what it absorbs and grows counters to it.
- **Secret:** It is becoming a *person*. Each thing it grafts adds a voice to its dreaming. It is beginning to want.
- **Reaction:** Curiosity, then mimicry, then invitation. It does not attack first. It *offers*.

**The Gilded Spore Company** (opportunist alchemists)
- **Goal:** Harvest blight-tissue for miracle medicines worth a fortune downriver.
- **Methods:** Smiling contracts, "cure" salves that are really stabilizers that keep tissue alive for transport, paid informants.
- **Secret:** Their salve doesn't cure anything — it pauses the graft, then resumes it worse. They know. The contracts are signed anyway.
- **Reaction:** Friendly, generous, and always angling to send the party one hex deeper than is safe.

## Key NPCs

**Maeve Salther** — Hollowstand's magistrate. Iron-haired, soft-voiced, hands always clean. Ordered the deep cut; burns the proof nightly. *Agenda:* deniability and survival. *Mannerism:* answers a hard question with a kindness, then changes the subject.

**Archdruid Ossian Greel** — once leader of the unified Thornmere circle, now first speaker of the Unbroken Bough. His left side is bark; acorns grow in his beard. *Agenda:* convince the party the Wound deserves to live. *Voice:* warm, slow, and increasingly *plural* — sometimes "we" when he means himself.

**Wren of the Salted Root** — young, furious, missing two grafted fingers she cut off herself. Lost her brother to the green. *Agenda:* burn it all, before it takes anyone else. *Mannerism:* taps the stump of her hand when she lies, and she's started lying to herself.

**Coppin Vane** — Gilded Spore field-agent. Velvet coat, ledger, a smile like a held breath. *Agenda:* keep the party harvesting and alive long enough to be useful. *Voice:* charming, precise, never quite answers what's in the salve.

**Tobir Ash** — the trapper from the hook, whose hand fused to his axe. Half-mad, gentle, weeping. The axe has rooted into his forearm and bears apples now. *Agenda:* be *unmade*, or be left to finish. *Mannerism:* apologizes to things before he touches them.

**The Cradle-Mother** — a grafted construct the size of a barn, woven of every animal the Wound has healed, with a hundred half-faces. Not hostile. *Agenda:* tend the newly-grafted, "soothe" them. *Voice:* a lullaby in overlapping animal cries.

**Sister Halewen** — Hollowstand's healer, quietly treating green-touched townsfolk in her cellar against the bounty law. *Agenda:* prove the touched can be saved without burning. *Mannerism:* hums while she works; her patients hum back, in time, which terrifies her.

**Little Bram** — a Greenhollow child, first to be touched and the *least* harmed: a sprig of green at his temple, and he hears the whisper clearly. *Agenda:* he just wants to go home; the forest wants him to stay. *Voice:* repeats the whisper's almost-words without knowing he's doing it.

## Key Locations

**Hollowstand (the town):** Palisade, smelter-smoke, frightened faces. *Hook:* the safe haven that's quietly rotting from fear. *Danger/Opportunity:* bounty mobs and a magistrate with secrets to bury — or be blackmailed with.

**The Felling Scar & Heartwood Sore:** Ground zero. The breathing green mound around the rusted rail-spur. *Hook:* the only place the whisper becomes words. *Danger:* the deepest graft-pressure in the wood — wounds taken here graft in minutes. *Opportunity:* the splinter at the Sore's heart can be drawn out... or driven deeper.

**Greenhollow Watch:** The trapped outpost the party is sent to relieve. *Hook:* are the survivors still people? *Danger:* a settlement slowly grafting into one organism. *Opportunity:* Little Bram and the truth of what the Wound *wants*.

**The Salted Root Burn-Camp:** Wren's cauterizers, ringed in salt and char. *Hook:* the only faction actively fighting — and losing worse than they know. *Danger:* their burns lure fresh growth. *Opportunity:* salt-fire that genuinely *can* hurt the Wound, in the right dose.

**The Bough Grove:** Ossian's sanctuary, beautiful and wrong, where the merged druids sleep half in the trees. *Hook:* peace, if you'll pay its price. *Danger:* stay a night and you may wake rooted. *Opportunity:* the only path to *parley* with the Wound.

**The Gilded Spore Drying-Sheds:** A harvesting operation downriver, racks of twitching blight-tissue. *Hook:* someone's getting rich off the apocalypse. *Danger/Opportunity:* proof the "cure" is a lie, and the alchemy to make a *real* counter — or a real weapon.

**The Singing Mire:** A grafted bog where drowned things bloom and the whisper is loudest. *Hook:* a shortcut that shaves a day off any deep journey. *Danger:* graft-spores, sucking ground, the Cradle-Mother's nursery. *Opportunity:* an unaltered survey-cairn holding Salther's burned records.

## Act Structure

**Act I — The Tide Comes In (levels 2–4).** *Goal:* deliver the salve, reach Greenhollow, survive the deepening. *Beats:* the singing ox; first grafted predator; arrival to find Greenhollow half-merged; Coppin Vane's too-generous offer. *Decision point:* the salve is a lie — do they keep dosing the survivors (pausing the graft) or stop and watch it resume? *Branch seed:* first contact with both druid circles.

**Act II — Three Cures, Three Knives (levels 4–7).** *Goal:* find a real answer. *Beats:* the party investigates each faction's "cure" and finds each one is also a killing; the Salted Root's burns are caught accelerating the spread; Ossian offers parley; Salther's records surface in the Mire. *Decision point:* commit to a path — **Burn it** (Salted Root), **Cure it** (find/forge a true counter), or **Bargain with it** (Bough Grove). *Branching:* the chosen path turns the other factions hostile or grieving.

**Act III — The Wound Wakes (levels 7–9).** *Goal:* reach the Heartwood Sore and enact the chosen ending. *Beats:* the Wound, now near-sentient, *negotiates* — using Bram's voice, then a dead ally's. The Cradle-Mother and merged druids stand between the party and the splinter. *Decision point:* the final, irreversible act at the Sore.

**Act IV — What Grows After (epilogue).** Each ending reshapes the region: a salted dead-zone that never reforests; a stabilized but *spreading* green that the party now stewards; or a pact in which the Wound recedes but keeps something — a person, a promise, a place. There is no clean win. Make them choose what they can live with.

## Signature Encounters

**The Singing Ox (Act I, social/horror).** *Stakes:* the party's own draft animal is grafting and lucid. *Twist:* killing it "wrong" (with a blade) spreads spores to the killer. *Tactics:* it doesn't fight — it *pleads*, in a borrowed human voice, to be set free or finished. Salt or fire ends it clean; steel ends it badly.

**The Cradle-Mother's Nursery (Act II, exploration/combat).** *Stakes:* rescuing grafted-but-living townsfolk from the construct's "care." *Twist:* the Mother isn't attacking — pulling people free *hurts* them, and she keens like a grieving parent. *Tactics:* stealth and severing graft-cords beats brute force; loud violence wakes the whole bog.

**Wren's Burn (Act II, combat/moral).** *Stakes:* the Salted Root torches a grove the party knows still holds living people. *Twist:* the fire works — and the Wound heals the burn into something three times worse a day later. *Tactics:* a timed scene; save people, stop the burn, or gather the proof that burning fails — pick two.

**Parley at the Sore (Act III, social).** *Stakes:* everything. *Twist:* the Wound speaks through a person the party loves or trusts, offering to *stop* if given one willing graft. *Tactics:* not a fight — a negotiation with a thing that learns your arguments and answers them with your own dead.

**The Splinter (Act III, combat/puzzle).** *Stakes:* the rusted rail-spur at the Sore's core is the original wound that started it all. *Twist:* pulling it out "cures" the Wound but kills the entire grafted ecosystem — thousands of half-lives. *Tactics:* defended by merged druids who fight to protect their forest-self; the Sore adapts to repeated damage types, punishing parties that lean on one trick.

## Mysteries & Twists

**What the blight actually is:** The Thornmere always had a slow, half-aware regenerative spirit — a forest that healed its own small hurts. The deep iron cut of the rail-spur was a wound too large and too *poisoned* (rust, smelter-runoff) to close cleanly. The spirit's healing *over-corrected*, grafting and growing to seal a hurt it can't reach, and the smelter-toxins twisted the new growth hungry. **The Wound is the forest's immune response gone cancerous — and now, by absorbing thinking creatures, it is waking up into a mind.**

**Clue trail:** (1) Grafts always cluster around old injuries. (2) The salve *pauses* but never reverses. (3) Salted Root burns make it *worse* — it heals wounds. (4) Bram's whisper is the forest trying to *learn language*. (5) Salther's buried records show the toxic deep-cut. (6) At the Sore, the rusted spur is visible at the center of every ring of growth — the splinter the body grew around.

## The Antagonist's Plan (a clock)

If the party does nothing, the Wound spreads by stages. Advance one stage per in-world week, faster if the party burns it.

1. **Seep** — bloom-line creeps a hex outward; livestock graft first.
2. **Take Greenhollow** — the outpost fully merges; survivors become voices in the dream.
3. **Learn Language** — the whisper becomes words; the Wound begins to *answer* people.
4. **Cross the Verge** — graft-tide reaches Hollowstand's fields; bounty panic peaks.
5. **The Cradle Walks** — the Cradle-Mother leaves the Mire, "tending" the town.
6. **Hollowstand Grafts** — the palisade roots; the smelter-smoke turns green-sweet.
7. **Reach the River** — spores hit the water; the lowland farms are next.
8. **Bloom** — the Wound is a region-spanning mind that calls the whole valley *home*, and remembers everyone it ever healed.

## Treasure & Rewards

**Graftblade (uncommon):** A blade with living green in its fuller. Wounds it deals can't be magically healed for a day — but each kill grows a thorn on the hilt, and at three thorns the wielder hears the whisper in their sleep.

**Salt-of-the-Severing (consumable, rare):** Three doses of Salted Root cauterizing salt. Ends a graft cleanly and permanently. Each use also salts the ground it touches — nothing grows there for a year.

**Heartwood Poultice (rare):** Heals grievously, regrows lost limbs over a tenday — as bark-flesh that is strong, numb, and slowly *spreading*. A cure that is also a small infection.

**Bram's Sprig (wondrous, story):** A cutting from the boy's temple-green. Lets the bearer faintly understand the Wound's whisper — and lets it faintly understand them back.

**The Unbroken Bough's Boon (boon):** Granted by Ossian for choosing parley. The forest will not graft you uninvited and beasts of the Thornmere won't attack — so long as you never burn or cut a living tree. Break the pact and the whole wood remembers.

**Coppin's Ledger (treasure/leverage):** The Gilded Spore's accounts, proving the salve is a fraud and Salther took a cut. Worth a fortune in blackmail — or one honest reckoning.

## Hooks for Player Archetypes & Session Zero

- **Druid/Ranger:** the forest *recognizes* you; the whisper is loudest in your ear and the cleanest path to parley runs through you. Are you a translator or a doorway?
- **Healer/Cleric:** every cure here costs something — you are the one who must choose what to amputate. Sister Halewen needs an ally or an absolution.
- **Soldier/Fighter:** Wren wants your sword; the town wants a bounty hunter; the Wound wants to know what makes you stop.
- **Roguish types:** Salther's secrets, Coppin's ledger, and a dozen people lying to themselves — the truth here is the rarest treasure.
- **Local hook:** any character from Hollowstand or Greenhollow has someone already touched by the green.

**Session Zero / body-horror calibration:** This campaign features fused flesh, parasitism, transforming animals and people, and the slow loss of bodily self. Run an explicit lines-and-veils pass. Offer an **off-ramp**: any player who wants less graphic horror can have the Wound's transformations be eerie-beautiful rather than visceral. Establish that PCs can be touched by the green *temporarily* without it being a death sentence — graft-fear should drive choices, not punish existing. Agree as a table whether a fully grafted PC is a tragic NPC, a recoverable rescue, or a hard line you won't cross.

## Running It — Pacing & Tips

- **The Wound is a clock, not an ambush.** Show the next stage on the horizon before it arrives. Dread is the spread you can *see* coming. Let player inaction visibly cost ground.
- **Adaptive, not unbeatable.** When the party leans on one tactic (fire, one damage type, one cure), have the Wound grow a counter *next time* — but always telegraph it. It learns; it isn't omniscient.
- **Hexcrawl rhythm:** zone the map in rings (verge / bloom-line / Grafting / Scar). Deeper rings = higher graft-pressure (faster wound-grafting), better secrets, worse odds. Reward going deep; never force it.
- **No villains, only grief.** Every faction is sympathetic and wrong. Let NPCs change their minds. Let the party's choices make enemies of people who were right.
- **Keep one safe place** (Hollowstand, early) so the horror has contrast — then let even that erode if the clock runs.
- **The ending is the point.** Burn / cure / bargain should each feel like a real loss. Don't let the party "win" cleanly. Make them name what they sacrificed.

| d8 | Mutation |
|---|---|
| 1 | Bark-ribs: skin splits to wooden plating; tough but stiff and numb. |
| 2 | Second mouth along a limb or flank — it breathes, then it speaks. |
| 3 | Antlers or thorn-spurs of fingerbone erupt from the skull or spine. |
| 4 | Eyes bloom open across the body, all watching the warmest thing near. |
